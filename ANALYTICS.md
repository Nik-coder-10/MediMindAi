# Administrative Morbidity & Operational Analytics

**AyurSetu Clinical Platform**
**Ministry of Ayush / All India Institute of Ayurveda (AIIA)**

---

## 📊 1. Overview & Epidemiological Value
The **AyurSetu Analytics Layer** aggregates intake volume, clinical triage urgency, language adoption, and classical Ayurvedic diagnostic patterns into actionable intelligence for hospital administration and epidemiological research.

---

## 🔒 2. Privacy & Data Minimization
- **Strictly Anonymized**: Analytics endpoints return zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or Protected Health Information (PHI).
- **Tokenized Identifiers**: Patient encounters are tracked via ephemeral token hashes (`#AIIA-104`) rather than names or ABHA numbers.
- **Aggregated Telemetry**: Real-time rollups compute intake completion rates, average questions answered, and red-flag trigger frequencies without logging sensitive transcripts.
